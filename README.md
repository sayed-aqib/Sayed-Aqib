## About

Data analyst and data scientist based in Calicut, Kerala. Final-year PG Diploma in Data Science & Analytics at the University of Calicut, on top of a BSc in Applied Statistics with Data Science.

I build systems that explain themselves. Most of my work sits where modelling meets data quality — the interesting problems usually turn out to be in the labels, not the loss curve.

- Currently building an analytics dashboard for a D2C health-food brand
- Fine-tuned DistilBERT to classify risk clauses in commercial contracts, then traced its weakest class back to contradictions in the expert annotations themselves
- Interested in healthcare and finance applications of ML
- Reach me at **sayedaqib31@gmail.com**

|---|---|---|
| **Legal Risk Clause Analyzer** | Classifies 10 risk clause types in commercial contracts with dual explainability. 0.828 accuracy / 0.806 macro-F1 on 5-fold CV over CUAD. | DistilBERT · SHAP · FastAPI · React |
| **Hayah Care Analytics** | Executive dashboard for a D2C brand — 15 business metrics plus a Meta ads budget module. *In progress.* | Python · Full-stack |
